Wild Sourced Profile
Our Chicken Jerky is a wholesome, protein-rich treat crafted from 100% human-grade chicken to provide your dog with the nutrition they deserve. Carefully prepared to retain its natural flavour and nutrients, this delicious jerky is an excellent source of lean protein, helping support muscle development, energy levels, and overall health.Naturally rich in essential amino acids and vital nutrients, Chicken Jerky also contains natural glucosamine, which helps promote healthy joints and mobility.
